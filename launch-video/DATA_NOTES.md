# Data notes

Every number on screen is DEMO DATA, written for the film. None of it is real Archetype data.
All of it lives in `src/data/demo.ts` and inside the screen replicas in `src/components/ui/`.

Real items on screen, taken from the dashboard repo:
- The list of 22 office countries (`competitive_intel/workspace/country-offices.json`).
- Feature names and what they do (Home, Website, Search, Email, Insights, Site Audit, ACI report and share links, PR Tracking, Business Inquiries, To Be Approved, PPM Statistics).

Claims that need Dat's confirmation before the film is shown:
- "Save $1,200 a year. No SEMrush subscription." and the bento's "$1,200 saved every year": figure supplied by Dat, not verified against an invoice.
- The bento's "90 automatic updates" and "6:00 / 12:00 / 18:00": from Dat's bento design; the code's daily run is once a day (07:13).
- Competitor initials (NK, TR, AO, BV, LM, QH, MG, KI, NE, HC, AF), city names, search queries, request names and people's initials are fictional.
