# Reference study: "Best AI SaaS Product Launch Video | Numtera" (95 s, 1280x720, 30 fps)

Studied frame by frame (contact sheets at 2 fps for the whole film, 10 fps strips on every transition) and measured with pixel tracking.
It uses the same music track as our film. Its brand reveal ("Meet Numtera") lands on the drop.

## Structure
| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0 to 7.9 | Problem as typewriter text, centred, with a caret. Faint product windows drift, blurred, around the edges. A word is selected (blue highlight), deleted, retyped. |
| 7.9 | Zoom-through: the camera dives into the letter "o" until its white counter fills the frame. |
| 8 to 9.9 | "Stop Managing tickets" blurs into focus (accent words in brand blue) on white with soft blue glows. |
| 9.9 to 15 | Full brand-blue field. "Meet" huge and blurred, settles, then shrinks fast to normal size; the logo icon pops in between "Meet" and the name. Then a tagline builds word by word. |
| 15 to 29 | The product rises from below in 3D perspective, edges dissolving into light; camera drifts and pushes in; a light sweep crosses the glass; a side panel types its steps. |
| 29 to 39 | "Agent approves it as {learning}": words spread apart and drop out until only {learning} remains, which morphs into a blue progress bar, which becomes a UI card. |
| 39 to 47 | "it turns the solution into [Reusable knowledge]": brackets close in until "[ ]", then a coloured shape wipes into the product. |
| 47 to 55 | Single big words ("Auto-resolved", "And"), then a sentence that zooms past the camera into the next UI shot. |
| 55 to 78 | Product feature run with macro typing in huge form fields, cursor clicks, cards connecting. |
| 78 to 86 | Contrast lines: "They close tickets" / "We Eliminate them". Logo icon, name types in. |
| 86 to 95 | Typewriter tagline and call to action, URL small below. |

## Measured motion
- Cuts: 12 visual breaks in 95 s (about 1.3 per 10 s). Everything else is continuous motion.
- UI rise: 58 percent of the travel in the first 4 frames (0.13 s), settled after about 20 frames (0.7 s), then a slow constant drift of about 1 px per frame that never stops. Exponential ease-out.
- Big word to normal size ("Meet"): a near hold that keeps creeping (about 0.5 percent per frame), then the shrink happens in 8 frames (0.27 s), ease in-out.
- Blur-in of a phrase: about 24 frames (0.8 s) from blurred to sharp.
- Word build: about 0.3 s per word; spaced-out words gather together as they arrive.
- Type: a neo-grotesk (Inter-like), medium weight, centred, single line, roughly 7 percent of frame height. Dark ink on white, white on colour. One accent word in brand colour.
- Backgrounds: white with soft brand glows that drift at the edges; a full brand-colour field for the brand reveal; dark navy for a few emphasis lines (we keep these light, per Dat's earlier note).

## What we adopt
Typewriter with selection, zoom-through a letter, blur-in, big word to normal, words spreading out to leave one key word, key word morphing into a UI element, closing brackets, coloured wipe, product in 3D with dissolving edges, exponential rise then endless drift, few hard cuts, centred medium-weight type with one accent word.
