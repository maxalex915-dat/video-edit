# Marketing Dashboard launch film: storyboard (v1, for approval)

90 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps. White theme. Superseded by the rendered board: storyboard/storyboard-v2.png (19 key frames).
The track's quiet build runs 0 to 9.2 s and the beat drops at 9.2 s, so the reveal sits on the drop.
Music: first 53.7 s of the supplied track, then its own ending (bar-aligned edit, no fade).

Look: dark teal canvas built from the dashboard's teal, the brand gradient (green, cyan, light blue) as light and glow,
the dashboard UI itself rendered white and crisp exactly as in the product. Inter Display for headlines (the dashboard's own face),
JetBrains Mono for small labels. Everything is drawn in code, no screenshots. All figures are demo data.

## Choices I made, push back if you disagree

1. The product first appears at 9.2 s, not within 3 s. The music's first 9 seconds are a quiet build, so the problem beat fits there and the reveal hits the drop. Showing the dashboard earlier would waste the drop.
2. Dark canvas, white UI. The dashboard itself is white; putting it on a dark brand canvas makes the cards and the gradient glow, which is the premium launch-film look you asked for.
3. I left out "$1,200 saved every year" (from the bento) and the 6:00 / 12:00 / 18:00 schedule. I could not verify the dollar figure in the code, and the daily update actually runs once, at 07:13. Tell me if you want the $1,200 back and confirm it is right.
4. All numbers on screen are demo data, close in scale to the real dashboard but not the same, listed in DATA_NOTES.md.
5. I could not study the three YouTube references (no download from this session). If you want the motion measured against them, drop the files into the repo.
