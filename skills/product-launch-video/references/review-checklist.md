# Review checklist (before every delivery)

Go through this with stills, not memory. Fix anything that fails before sending.

## Picture
- [ ] Every scene: first text readable by frame 3; no two headlines on screen at once.
- [ ] No text or number cut by a card edge or the frame; no overlapping cards (check full-size stills of every
      UI-heavy scene).
- [ ] Only brand colours + white in grounds and highlights; no dark or foreign hue.
- [ ] Fluid ground visibly moving (compare two stills 1 s apart).
- [ ] Glass elements are see-through (the ground's colour shows through).
- [ ] No dead time: nothing new for > 1 s without voice; check the end of every scene.
- [ ] Payoff words stay up >= 1 s.
- [ ] Cursors: unhurried, land on their targets, every cursor does something visible.
- [ ] Hover lifts and drags ease in and out (no snapping).
- [ ] Logo: correct colours at every frame of the outro, never cut, wordmark aligned.
- [ ] Copied-reference scenes compared side by side with the reference at matched times.

## Sound
- [ ] Every voice line starts on its scene, with a breath before it; no clipped word ends.
- [ ] On-screen text lands on its spoken word (measured, not guessed).
- [ ] Each pop on a first visible frame; each swoosh leads its motion; no sound without an event.
- [ ] Music drop on the reveal; the track's own ending on the logo; nothing cut at the end.
- [ ] Mix at -14 LUFS, voice clearly on top.

## Delivery message
- [ ] Version, length, file.
- [ ] Changes by timestamp, plain words, short.
- [ ] What could not be done fully, and the next option.
- [ ] Open items: unverified claims and on-screen demo figures, music licence, lines to re-record.
